# Ticket 07: drag and monitor placement manual acceptance

Run Hood on Windows with the Release build.

- Click Hood without moving the pointer and confirm it reacts; drag it past the system drag threshold and confirm the drag does not also react or wake Hood.
- Drag the sleeping pet and confirm both Hood and its area move while he remains asleep.
- With roaming disabled, drag Hood freely. Confirm the area follows it and the location persists after Quit and relaunch.
- Drag Hood between monitors with different scaling and confirm the whole pet remains visible on the destination monitor.
- Drag to each work-area edge and confirm Hood and the roaming area stay accessible above the taskbar.
- Disconnect the monitor containing Hood. Confirm he moves into usable primary-monitor space and that placement is saved.
