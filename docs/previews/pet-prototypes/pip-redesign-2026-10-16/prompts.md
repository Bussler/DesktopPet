# Pip concept prompts

## Curious Pip

Output: `01-curious.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one static base sprite concept for Pip, an existing Windows desktop pet.
Input image: the supplied Pip sheet is a CHARACTER IDENTITY reference only, not a layout to reproduce. Use its orange red fox fur, cream muzzle/chest/tail tip, dark brown paws, large pointed ears, bushy tail, and forest-green wool shawl with short fringed ends.
Primary request: redesign Pip to feel like a specific living character with personality and emotional warmth. One fox only, one calm standing idle pose, full body entirely visible including ears, feet and tail. Upright small anthropomorphic fox with compact legs, suitable to read clearly at about 190 pixels tall.
Style/medium: polished hand-drawn 2D game sprite, expressive character design, clean warm dark-brown outlines, simple controlled painted shading, readable silhouette. Keep fur details purposeful.
Composition: centered on a square transparent canvas, character fills about 85% of the height, comfortable padding on all sides. Truly transparent background and clean alpha edges, no cast shadow.
Constraints: retain the forest-green wool shawl as his only clothing/accessory. Convey personality through silhouette, eyebrows, eyelids, mouth, ear angles, hands and posture. Natural attentive gaze with small restrained eye highlights; avoid the vacant symmetrical huge glassy doll eyes of the old reference.
No animation, no sequence, no sprite sheet, no duplicate foxes, no labels, no lettering, no watermark, no scenery, no props, no ground.
Direction 1 - CURIOUS LITTLE EXPLORER: a lively, slightly lean fox with a soft triangular face and an untidy forehead tuft. Warm amber eyes of moderate size, looking attentively at the viewer, one eyebrow subtly raised. Slight head tilt, one ear pricked and the other angled outward. A small open corner smile with a hint of delighted curiosity. His shoulders and weight shift gently to one side; one dark paw loosely holds the shawl near his chest, the other relaxed near his belly. Big flowing white-tipped tail curves up behind one side. Energetic and approachable without exaggerated baby proportions. Expression should suggest he has just noticed something interesting. Create ONLY this one direction.
```
