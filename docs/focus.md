# Focus and breaks

Open **Focus** from the pet's right-click menu or the tray menu. Both restore the same timer, including when pet sprites are hidden.

1. Choose focus and break lengths in whole minutes (1–1440). Defaults are **25/5**. Click **Start focus**; Hood keeps behaving normally.
2. At zero, Hood waves continuously. Click him to stop the wave and bring Focus forward without toggling Notes. Click **Take break** to begin your full break; you can also use that button directly.
3. Hood displays his sleep animation throughout the break. Clicking him brings Focus forward without waking him or toggling Notes. This appearance does not grant sleep recovery; ordinary pet care still works underneath it.
4. At zero, Focus appears silently with **Break finished**. Hood stays asleep until you manually **Start focus** again. Repeat for as many rounds as you like. Neither timer starts automatically.

**Pause/Resume** freezes/continues either countdown. **Skip break** finishes the break early and waits for Start focus, keeping Hood visually asleep. **Stop** returns to idle and removes timer animations. Closing Focus also stops the cycle; minimizing keeps it running. All timer reminders are silent.

**Always on top** affects only Focus. Durations and this choice remain available when you close/reopen Focus during the app run. Editing durations affects future intervals, never the current countdown. Restarting Hood restores 25/5 and always-on-top off. Computer sleep discards the cycle; it does not resume on wake. No Focus history or preferences are saved to disk.

## Notifications

This build provides **manual notification control**. Click **Open Windows notification settings** to manage Windows Do not disturb. Enable it for focused work and disable it for breaks yourself. Hood never enables, disables, or restores that manual setting, including on Pause, Stop, close, or exit.

Windows exceptions for priority apps, calls, reminders, and alarms still apply. This does not change Teams/Discord presence, mute audio, or block applications. Automatic Windows Focus access requires Microsoft's restricted app permission, which is unavailable for this build. The timer works independently of notification settings.
