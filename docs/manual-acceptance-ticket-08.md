# Ticket 08: roaming-area editor manual acceptance

Run Hood on Windows with the Release build.

- Open Hood's right-click menu and choose **Edit roaming area**. Confirm the normal desktop has no outline before or after edit mode.
- Drag the editor's top bar in both directions. Confirm the area moves and Hood remains fully inside it.
- Drag the lower-right handle smaller than Hood's footprint; confirm the area stops at the minimum size and does not clip the pet.
- Expand and move the area near the work-area edges; confirm it stays within the usable display above the taskbar.
- Choose **Done** and confirm the editor controls and outline close.
- Repeat with roaming disabled and while Hood is asleep; confirm editing remains available and does not wake him.
- Quit and relaunch; confirm the edited area and Hood placement persist.
